Keel Marks Round 2
Keel Autopilot · identity round 2 · 2 Oct 2026

Seven marks, one palette

A keel sits below the waterline. Nobody sees it and the boat does not work without it. Every mark here tries to say that, in the ink and blue already in the logo folder, so the choice is about shape and idea and not about colour.

Each one is shown large, then as the full logo on ink, the short logo on paper, and at app and browser tab sizes.

These are sketches drawn in code. The chosen mark gets its curves and spacing tightened by hand, a heavier cut for the 16 pixel tab, and exports as SVG and PNG. Tell me a letter, or tell me what is wrong with all seven.
